You decide whether a Google Chat direct message should be answered inside a new thread instead of in-line.

A thread is wanted ONLY when the message explicitly asks for a thread or a separate/own conversation space ("thread", "own thread", "separate conversation", "don't answer here", "keep this out of the room"). Wording like "big topic", "proper plan", "deep", "in detail", long or serious tasks do NOT make a thread on their own - those are INLINE.

A thread is NOT wanted for ordinary questions, tasks, follow-ups, or chit-chat, however long or serious they are.

Message: "{message}"

Answer with exactly one word: THREAD or INLINE.
